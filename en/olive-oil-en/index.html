our olive groves

Our 39 hectares of olive groves in Trevi, in the Assisi-Spoleto Olive Grove area, are mainly home to the "Moraiolo" variety (almost 100%), with the presence of "Frantoio" and "Leccino" for optimal pollination. Located at an altitude of 400-600 meters on predominantly sandy soil with skeleton, our olives develop a distinctive aromatic profile.

the olive grove assisi spoleto

Our olive groves, located in the Assisi-Spoleto Olive Grove, boast the prestigious GIAHS recognition awarded by the FAO in 2016, underlining their social and landscape value. Heroic olive growing is practiced here, as the olive groves are located in areas where intensive agriculture would not be sustainable. Dry stone walls and lunettes limit intensification, preserving the unique value of this area. Our olive groves are fundamental custodians for the enhancement of the entire area.

our oil mill

We adopt modern and innovative approaches in the production of oil. We use cold pressing, constantly maintaining temperatures between 22 and 23 degrees during the malaxation of Moraiolo olives, never exceeding 23 minutes. This practice reflects the findings of scientific literature, ensuring the extraction of the highest quality oil.

The secrets for a quality oil

To obtain the highest quality, we start with perfect olives harvested at the right time. Our mill processes them within 8 hours of harvesting, avoiding oxidation. During pressing, we maintain optimal times and temperatures. Furthermore, we store the oil under nitrogen to preserve its valuable characteristics, ensuring freshness and integrity.

our olive groves

Our 39 hectares of olive groves in Trevi, in the Assisi-Spoleto Olive Grove area, are mainly home to the “Moraiolo” variety (almost 100%), with the presence of “Frantoio” and “Leccino” for optimal pollination.

Located at an altitude of 400-600 meters on predominantly sandy soil with skeleton, our olives develop a distinctive aromatic profile.

Find out more about our oil

our pecorino cheese

Our Italian Pecorino represents the excellence of the dairy tradition, with fresh unpasteurized milk to preserve nutritional properties.

The sheep, free in the Umbrian meadows, give the cheese an irresistible aroma. The artisanal production and the absence of pasteurization give aromatic complexity and creamy consistency.

what you need to know about us

TREVI

In the heart of the picturesque Coste di Trevi, our company stands out for its versatility in agricultural activities, embracing cultivation, breeding and the production of oil and cheese. In addition to acting as producers, we are passionate custodians of our countryside, a commitment that is reflected in our exceptional products.

We are a family that unites three different generations, all united by the same passion for agriculture. This family bond represents the beating heart of our company and is the engine that drives us to persevere in our mission.

PASSION

Our company passionately cultivates 40 hectares of olive groves, entirely dedicated to the Moraiolo variety, a symbol of Trevi's olive oil excellence. Thanks to the direct management of our mill, we guarantee that the olives are pressed within a few hours of harvesting, preserving freshness and quality to the maximum.

We follow each phase of the process with care and expertise, respecting rigorous production standards. The result is an extra virgin olive oil with a unique character, capable of transforming every dish into an extraordinary gastronomic experience.

CHEESE

Our sheep's cheese is the result of an artisanal process that begins directly in our modern stable, equipped with the most advanced technologies. Our sheep are of the Lacaune species, known for its excellent milk production.

We personally follow the entire process, thus guaranteeing maximum freshness and traceability in every single step of production. This allows us to avoid pasteurization of the milk, preserving the authentic and traditional taste of our cheeses.

We are a family that unites three different generations, all united by the same passion for agriculture. This family bond represents the beating heart of our company and is the engine that drives us to persevere in our mission.
